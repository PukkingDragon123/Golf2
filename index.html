❔ How to Play

Every shot, three steps

  • 1. Club: pick Driver (distance), Wedge (high & soft) or Putter (roll) from the left — or keys 1 / 2 / 3. Each shows its max distance.
  • 2. Power: drag the power bar up (or hold SPACE) and release to lock how hard you swing.
  • 3. Accuracy: a marker sweeps a bar — tap / SPACE to stop it in the green zone. Miss left/right and the shot pushes that way.
  • Aim & look: drag the view (or A/D). The glowing arc previews your shot.
  • Jet boost: with the Pulse Jet equipped, tap in flight to boost on.

The worlds

  • 🌍 Verdant Links — wind & water, your warm-up.
  • 🌙 Lunar Links — 1/6 gravity & no air; drives fly forever.
  • 🦕 Jurassic Fairway — thick air, lava & roaming dinos.
  • 🪐 Orbital Open — gravity wells bend your ball; the void keeps strays.
  • 👽 Xeno Country Club — springy turf, launch fungi & geysers.

Progress

  • Beat a world to unlock the next. Earn 🪙 by scoring well.
  • Spend coins at the 3D Xeno Gear Emporium on clubs, balls & cart tech.
