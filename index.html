❔ How to Play

Fling it!

  • Drag back from the ball like a slingshot — further pull = more power.
  • Aim by the angle you pull; the glowing arc shows where it'll go.
  • Release to fling. Your potato winds up and whacks it — with a little chaos for fun.
  • Mouse or finger. Pass the device around between turns.

🏃 Ball Dash

  • Everyone runs at once — first potato to the ball hits first!
  • Alternate your two keys to sprint: P1 A/S · P2 F/G · P3 H/J · P4 K/L.
  • On touch, hammer your colour pad at the bottom.

The party

  • Build your spud in the Studio — name, colour, hat & ball skin.
  • 💥 Long Bomb · 🎯 Pin Seeker · 🏁 Hole Rush
  • ⭐ Star Smash · 🎯 Bullseye · 🔁 Take Two · 🏃 Ball Dash
  • Place high each round for points. Most points wins 👑.
  • Earn 🏆 achievements to unlock new hats, ball skins & colours.
