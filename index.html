❔ How to Play

Take your shot

  • Aim: drag the view or press A/D (← →).
  • Loft / club: W/S (↑ ↓) — higher loft = higher, shorter shots.
  • Spin: E/Q for back/top spin, Z/X to curve left/right.
  • Power: hold SPACE — the meter sweeps up — release to swing.
  • Jet boost: with the Pulse Jet equipped, tap SPACE in flight.
  • The glowing arc previews your shot. Watch the wind & gravity gauges.

The worlds

  • 🌍 Verdant Links — wind & water, your warm-up.
  • 🌙 Lunar Links — 1/6 gravity & no air; drives fly forever.
  • 🦕 Jurassic Fairway — thick air drags shots; lava & roaming dinos.
  • 🪐 Orbital Open — gravity wells bend your ball; the void keeps strays.
  • 👽 Xeno Country Club — springy turf, launch fungi & updraft geysers.

Progress

  • Beat a world to unlock the next. Earn 🪙 by scoring well.
  • Spend coins in the Gear Shop on unique upgrades for your cart.
