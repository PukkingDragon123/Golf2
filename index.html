❔ How to Play

Fling it!

  • Drag back from the ball like a slingshot — the further you pull, the more power.
  • Aim by the angle you pull; the glowing arc shows where it'll go.
  • Release to fling. Your golfer winds up and whacks it — with a little chaos for fun.
  • Works with mouse or finger. Pass the device around between turns.

The party

  • 1–4 players, each with a name, colour & goofy hat.
  • 💥 Long Bomb — furthest fling wins.
  • 🎯 Pin Seeker — closest to the flag wins.
  • 🏁 Hole Rush — sink it in the fewest swings.
  • Place high each round to earn points. Most points after 5 rounds takes the crown 👑.
